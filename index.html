Pricing — Your Story, At Your Own Pace

Simple, transparent pricing for our done-for-you service

Setup

$175 AUD, one-off

We handle all the technical bits — getting your own private site online, connecting secure photo storage, and setting up your contact page. No accounts to figure out, no passwords to lose track of, no tech headaches. Just a finished, working site with your name on it.

Writing

$15 AUD per chapter

Send us a few sentences about a memory, an event, a person who mattered — whatever you'd like to write about next. We'll turn it into a polished chapter on your site. Billed in small batches as you go, so there's never a big bill waiting at the end.

Go at your own pace.

There's no deadline here. Some people finish their story in a few months. Others take a year or more, working through memories a little at a time. Whatever your pace, we're here whenever you're ready for the next chapter.

A typical 20–30 chapter autobiography: setup + $300–$450 in chapters = around $475–$625 (AUD), plus ongoing website hosting. Hosting is a separate monthly cost.

Optional extras — photo galleries, audio narration, and other personal touches — can be added any time, priced individually once you know what you'd like. Website hosting is an ongoing cost, separate from the one-off setup fee and chapter writing. For example, Peter's own autobiography is hosted with Render at a cost of about $25 per month. Your hosting cost may be different, depending on the service and requirements. We'll explain the price and get your agreement before arranging anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm not very good with computers — is this still for me? +

Yes. Choose the personal-help option and Peter can take care of the technical setup with you. You can concentrate on your memories and stories. The free download is intended for people who are comfortable following software and website setup instructions.

What if I want to stop after a few chapters? +

That's completely fine. You only pay for the chapters you've written. There's no minimum, no contract, and no pressure to finish a certain number. Your site is yours to grow whenever — and however much — you'd like.

How long does it take? +

That's really up to you. Some people write a chapter every week; others take a few months between chapters while memories come back to them. We never rush you — take all the time you need.

What do I need to send you for each chapter? +

Just a few sentences about a memory, event, or person — as much or as little detail as you're comfortable with. We'll ask a few gentle follow-up questions if we need more, and turn what you give us into a written chapter.

Who owns the finished site? +

It is your story and your content. The website address and hosting arrangements will be discussed with you as part of setup.

Can I add photos? +

Yes — a photo gallery is available as an optional extra, priced separately once you know what you'd like included.

Do I need to arrange hosting? +

Hosting is simply the service that keeps your autobiography website on the internet, so family and friends can visit it using its web address. It is an ongoing cost, a bit like a monthly phone or internet bill, and is not included in the $175 setup fee. For example, Peter's own autobiography is hosted with Render and costs about $25 per month. The cost for your website may be different, depending on the hosting service and what your site needs. Peter will explain the likely cost and get your agreement before anything is arranged. You won't have to sort out the technical details yourself.

What if I get stuck or have a question partway through? +

Just get in touch. We're happy to help at any stage — that's part of the service, not something you'll be charged extra for.
